Keeping Up With the Macs SMS Consent & Opt-In

SMS Notification Consent Form

Document Version 1.0  ·  Effective September 1, 2026

Keeping Up With the Macs ("we," "us," or "our") sends operational SMS text message notifications to enrolled team members. This page serves as our official opt-in consent record for our internal messaging program.

This form must be completed by any team member who will receive SMS notifications from Keeping Up With the Macs. Completion of this form constitutes written consent to receive text messages as described below.

What Messages You Will Receive

By opting in, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Keeping Up With the Macs that may include:

  • Financial bill payment alerts and confirmations
  • Project management task reminders and status updates
  • Business automation and workflow notifications
  • Internal platform alerts relevant to your role or account
  • Messages may include relevant URLs and support phone numbers

These messages are strictly operational and internal. No marketing or promotional messages will be sent through this program.

Important Disclosures

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and operational events. You may receive multiple messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier SMS and data rates may apply to all messages sent and received. Contact your wireless carrier for details.

Your mobile number will not be shared. We will not share, sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number with any third party for marketing purposes.

How to Opt Out or Get Help

You may withdraw your consent and stop receiving messages at any time:

  • Reply STOP (or CANCEL, QUIT, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, REVOKE, or END) to any message
  • Contact us at support@keepingupwiththemacs.com

For help, reply HELP to any message or email us at support@keepingupwiththemacs.com.

To re-enroll after opting out, reply START, YES, or UNSTOP to any previous message.

Consent is Voluntary

You are not required to consent to receive SMS messages as a condition of employment or access to any services provided by Keeping Up With the Macs. Opting out will not affect your employment status or access to non-SMS services.
