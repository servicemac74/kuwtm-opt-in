Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Keeping Up With the Macs ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, employees, and business contacts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information, including any information collected in connection with our SMS and text messaging programs.

By using our services or providing your mobile phone number, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, and business contact information
  • Mobile phone number (when provided for SMS notifications)
  • Usage data and interaction logs related to our internal platforms
  • Financial and project management data submitted through our tools

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Send internal operational SMS notifications, including project management alerts, financial task reminders, and automation status updates
  • Administer and improve our services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Communicate important account or service updates

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

Mobile Number Non-Sharing Statement: We will not share, sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes under any circumstances.

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to our SMS program, you consent to receive text messages from Keeping Up With the Macs. These messages are limited to internal operational notifications, including but not limited to:

  • Financial bill payment alerts and reminders
  • Project management and task status updates
  • Business automation status notifications

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your account activity and operational events. You may receive up to several messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier message and data rates may apply to all SMS messages sent and received. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

How to Opt Out: You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent to your number.

How to Get Help: Reply HELP to any of our messages or contact us directly at support@keepingupwiththemacs.com for assistance.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist in delivering our platform and messaging services, bound by confidentiality obligations
  • As required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Keeping Up With the Macs, our users, or the public

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Mobile phone numbers collected for SMS notifications are retained only while the individual remains an active participant in the messaging program.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of certain data uses, including SMS communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

8. Third-Party Services

Our messaging platform may use third-party SMS delivery providers (such as Twilio) to facilitate message delivery. These providers act as data processors on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations. They are not authorized to use your information for their own purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our services following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
